Postrinch is a program you run on your own computer. It signs in to the accounts you add (Gmail and Google Calendar, and any mail account with IMAP and SMTP, such as Fastmail, iCloud or a work server), downloads their mail and calendars into your home folder, and carries out what you do: read, search, file, send, answer invitations. Updates

Postrinch, installed with postrinch --install, keeps itself current. Once a day it asks GitHub whether there is a new version of Postrinch and, if there is, downloads it from Postrinch's releases on GitHub, checks that we signed it, and keeps it ready. Nothing changes until you choose "Restart to update". A download whose signature doesn't check out is never used. You can always download a release yourself from GitHub instead.

We may change, add or remove features in new versions, and may stop making new ones. Postrinch keeps working on your computer as long as your providers let it sign in.
