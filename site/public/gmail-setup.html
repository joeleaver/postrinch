Set up Gmail sign-in

About ten minutes, once per computer.

Postrinch signs in to Google the way Google asks desktop apps to: in your browser, with a Google "client" that names the app. Until Postrinch has passed Google's review, its own client can sign in only a handful of testers, so for now you make a client of your own. It costs nothing, it's yours alone, and your mail still goes only between your computer and Google.

1. Make a Google Cloud project

  1. Open the Google Cloud console and sign in with any Google account (it needn't be one you'll read mail from).
  2. Create a project and give it a name you'll recognise, such as My Postrinch.

2. Turn on the Gmail and Calendar APIs

  1. In the project, open APIs & Services › Library.
  2. Find and Enable the Gmail API, then the Google Calendar API.

3. Describe your app to Google

  1. Open Google Auth Platform (also reached as APIs & Services › OAuth consent screen) and choose Get started.
  2. App name: anything, such as My Postrinch. Support and contact email: your own.
  3. Audience: External.
  4. Under Audience › Test users, add every Gmail address you'll add to Postrinch.
  5. Under Data access, add these scopes (paste each into "Manually add scopes"):
    • https://mail.google.com/: your mail
    • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar: your calendars
    • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.basic: optional, so rules can run on Gmail itself

4. Make the client

  1. Open Clients and choose Create client.
  2. Application type: Desktop app. Any name.
  3. Choose Download JSON on the client you just made.

5. Give it to Postrinch

Save the file you downloaded as ~/.config/postrinch/google-oauth-client.json:

mkdir -p ~/.config/postrinch
mv ~/Downloads/client_secret_*.json ~/.config/postrinch/google-oauth-client.json

Start Postrinch (or close it and start it again), open Settings › Accounts and choose Add Google account. Your browser opens on Google's sign-in.

Google will say it hasn't verified this app. That app is the one you just made: choose Continue, and allow what it asks. Do the same for each account.

6. Keep it signed in

While your project's audience is in Testing, Google ends its sign-ins after seven days, and Postrinch will show the account as needing to sign in again. To stop that, open Audience and choose Publish app. You don't need to submit it for verification: a project used by you alone counts as personal use. Google keeps showing its warning when you sign in, and that's all.

If something goes wrong

  • "Sync is off: Google sign-in is not set up on this computer." The file isn't at ~/.config/postrinch/google-oauth-client.json, or Postrinch was running when you put it there. Check the name and restart Postrinch.
  • "The Google sign-in settings could not be read." The file isn't a Desktop app client. Make one of type Desktop app (step 4) and download it again.
  • Google says "Access blocked". The address you're signing in with isn't among the test users (step 3), or the project isn't published yet and that list is the only way in.
  • Postrinch refuses the sign-in. It needs both mail and calendar. Sign in again and leave both ticked.
